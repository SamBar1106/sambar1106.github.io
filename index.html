FAQ

Who is Loom for?

Small local shops and online sellers who want one simple place to run their store.

Can Loom customize my site or connect my other tools?

Yes. We work with a limited number of shops, so we can do custom work and integrations with you.

How do I get paid?

Through Stripe. Connect your Stripe account in Loom and payouts go to your bank.

Do my customers need an account?

No. They can browse and check out as guests.

What does it cost?

2.9% + 30¢ per sale, card processing included.

Does Loom store card numbers?

No. Stripe handles all card details.

Does Loom use AI?

Not yet. AI tools for SEO and marketing are coming soon.
